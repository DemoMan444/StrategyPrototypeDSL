Project: Metamodel For Prototyping Strategy Games in a Text-Based format to play the strategies through and generate some reusable code for Unity

(old topic name, same project: DSL for Prototyping Text-Based Strategy Games)

 

2-3 representative examples
 

Stronghold Crusader (RTS/Economy + Warfare)
Resources (Wood, Stone, Gold, Food, Population)
Actions
Gather resources
Build structures
Hire units
Attack enemies
Defend your castle/lands
Events
Disease
Enemy raids
Fire
Rules
Population limited by housing
Workforce limited by population
Buildings require resources to be built/some to be operated
Resources are only produced by some building aside from starting resources
Conditions
Win Precondition: destroy enemies economy/castle so they cannot recover in time for a next attack
Win: kill enemy lord
Lose Precondition: castle destroyed/economy/population gone
Lose: your lord gets killed
AI Behavior
Enemy lords with strategies (rather small variation: how what to build and how often to attack/help allies)
Rewards/Punishments
Killing enemy lords give some resource benefits but mostly the benefit of having less enemies to annoy you
Running out of food in granary lowers population
Statistics
Live score tracking- score consists of only Millitary units nr-s (can be decisive as many weak units seem thus stronger than fewer strong though not) and Gold (again can be decisive as maybe the gold was just invested into better economy or millitary)
 

Civilization IV (Turn-based Strategy)
Resources
Gold, Production, Food, Science, Culture
Forest- can be chopped down for production boost
Natural resources(gold, gems, etc)
Actions
Build cities
Research technology
Diplomacy
Spread religion (possible diplomatic, economic advantage)
Control military units
Control worker units
Rules
Turn-based progression
Tech-tree dependencies
Events
Random world events
Diplomacy changes over time either positive or negative direction based on players actions
Conditions
Win condition: World Domination by conquering enough cities, controlling enough diplomacy, economy and resources
AI
Strategic empire decision making based on AI leaders traits. For example agressive leader wants to dominate while Financial leader aims for optimizing personal economy. So big difference.
Statistics
Live score tracking- score consists of all your resources diplomacy etc
After game score tracking over time
